03R&D equipment controls and commissioning

Onsemi R&D · Role: Owned controls · Scope: One new furnaceIn service

Challenge

The R&D team needed a new experimental furnace that runs a flammable process gas. Safety had to come first.

Engineering approach

I owned the controls work. I built the PLC logic and HMI, built closed-loop PID gas control, designed gas-detection safety interlocks and fail-safes, and helped the team commission the furnace. I also worked with the fire department. Gas safety limits for the area came from an independent third-party assessment.

Outcome

No control defects found in commissioning or production runs, and the project came in on time and under budget.

Core competenciesPLC/HMI DevelopmentPID ControlGas SafetyCommissioning

04Manufacturing execution system integration

Onsemi · Corporate initiative · Role: Controls lead on a cross-team project · Scope: Every furnaceIn service

Challenge

Recipes were moved onto each machine by hand, and several versions existed, so the wrong one could get loaded. Corporate asked for a fix.

Engineering approach

I built the controls side with the MOS team and process engineering, integrating the PLC and HMI with MES so each run automatically gets the approved recipe from a single controlled source.

Outcome

It runs on every furnace in the fleet, with one source of truth for recipes.

Core competenciesPLC/MES IntegrationHMIStandardizationCross-Team Delivery
