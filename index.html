Controls for a hydrogen-process R&D furnace

Onsemi · R&D · Controls EngineerIn service

The problem

The team needed a new R&D furnace that uses hydrogen to study crystal quality. With a flammable process gas, safety had to come first.

What I did

I owned the controls work. I built the PLC logic and HMI, built closed-loop PID gas control, designed gas-detection safety interlocks and fail-safes, and helped the team commission the furnace. I also worked with the fire department. Gas safety limits for the area came from an independent third-party assessment.

How it went

No control defects found in commissioning or production runs. The $100K+ project came in on time and under budget.

Recipe safeguards that stopped aborted runs

Onsemi · Process ReliabilityIn service

The problem

Hardware limits could be changed without the matching recipe settings being updated. When the two disagreed, a motion axis could stall mid-run and the run had to be aborted.

What I did

I added a check that validates each recipe step against the machine's limits before it runs, and an automatic return to a home position after every run.

How it went

That cause of aborted runs went away, saving a five-figure sum on every run it would have hit.

Centralized recipe management

Onsemi · Corporate Initiative · With MOS, MES and Process EngineeringIn service

The problem

Recipes were moved onto each machine by hand, and several versions existed, so the wrong one could get loaded.

What I did

I built the controls side with the MOS team and process engineering. MES tells the PLC which recipe a run needs, and the operator screen pulls the approved version from a central server.

How it went

It runs on every furnace in the fleet, with one source of truth for recipes.
